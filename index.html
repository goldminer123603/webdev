01. Service Tier Packages & Core Pricing Architecture

Selecting the correct technology tier is critical for matching your operational budget with your technical requirements. KTakour Tech Solutions structures all builds into three main engineering tiers:

  • Basic Starter (1,000 GHS): Ideal for emerging businesses, startups, or simple product launches needing a fast online landing footprint and formal corporate branding documents.
  • Intermediate Pro (3,000 GHS): Designed for established companies requiring dynamic multi-page user experiences, complete visual vector brand assets, and custom search engine visibility.
  • Full Enterprise (5,000 GHS): Engineered for robust web systems, database integrations, SaaS prototypes, custom logic tools, payment gateways, and dedicated 30-day technical support.

04. Brand Identity & Vector Design Standards

Branding is not just a visual layer; it is your business's visual trust foundation. Depending on your package, our visual asset delivery includes:

  • Vector Scalability: SVG and EPS master files that remain razor-sharp at any size, from digital mobile headers to billboard print media.
  • Color System Definition: Exact HEX, RGB, and CMYK color palettes customized for high-contrast dark-mode and light-mode environments.
  • Typography Rules: Selection of clean, web-safe, and modern variable fonts optimized for quick reading and modern UI standards.
